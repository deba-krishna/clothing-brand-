Frequently Asked Questions

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy on all items. Products must be unworn and in original packaging. Shipping costs are covered by the customer for returns.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within India. International orders typically take 10-15 business days depending on the destination.

Do you offer size exchanges?

Yes! If you receive the wrong size, we'll gladly exchange it at no extra cost. Contact our customer support team for assistance.

Are your products sustainable?

We're committed to sustainability. All our products are made with eco-friendly materials and ethical manufacturing practices.

Can I bulk order?

Yes! We offer bulk discounts for corporate orders and events. Please email us at hello@noirwear.com for special pricing.

Do you have a physical store?

Currently we operate online only, but you can visit our showroom by appointment. Contact us for scheduling.
